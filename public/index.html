      Get directions where a plant's exact site is known, or report a problem so staff can check it.

    What the data can — and can't — tell you

    • Locations are approximate. The spreadsheet lists each plant's area, not its exact site, so most results point to an area centre.

    • Status is listed, not verified. Every plant is marked "Fully Functional" in the source, with no date. Staff verification is shown separately when it exists.

    • Capacity isn't an allowance. Gallons per hour is the plant's production rate — not how much one person can collect.

    • Ratings aren't safety tests. Water-test evidence and community ratings are kept apart. No test on record means "Unknown".
