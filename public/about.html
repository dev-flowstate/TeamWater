Where area positions come from

To rank plants by distance we need a rough position for each area. Our area list (gazetteer) has 49 entries, but only 17 have a usable position: 11 areas, 5 towns and the airport. The rest are flagged for staff to review and position by hand.

Every area position is approximate. We draw it as a circle, never as a pin, and never present it as a plant's exact site. Plants whose area has no position can't be ranked by distance — search for their area or town by name to see them in a text list.

Area positions: Who's On First (https://whosonfirst.org/docs/licenses/), including data from GeoNames (CC BY, https://www.geonames.org/) and Wikidata (CC0). Tehsil checks: geoBoundaries (ODbL). How distances are worked out

  • Approximate straight-line distanceFor plants with an exact site: the distance "as the crow flies" from the place you chose. Real journeys are longer.
  • Approximate distance to area centreFor plants listed only by area: the distance to the rough centre of that area. The plant itself could be anywhere in the area.
  • Travel distance by roadOnly when a routing service is switched on and answering, and only for plants with an exact site. If it isn't available we say so and fall back to straight-line distance — we never guess a route.

"Nearest" orders plants by these distances (ties are broken by plant ID, so the order is always the same). "Recommended" also weighs verified status, dated test evidence, confirmed problems and ratings, and explains its reasons on each card. Your search location is used to answer the request and is not stored.

Water quality evidence is not a rating

Water quality evidence

Comes only from recorded laboratory tests, with the sample date and the standard used. It is historical: a past test doesn't guarantee the water today. With no test on record we say "Unknown" — never "safe".

Community experience rating

Stars from people who used the plant — about queues, cleanliness of the site, and service. It is not a water-safety measure. Below 3 ratings we show "Too few ratings", and scores are adjusted so a handful of votes can't dominate.

Community reports are unverified until reviewed

Anyone can report a problem with a plant. A report is not a finding: it never changes a plant's official status by itself. Plant cards show how many unverified reports are under review, separately from issues staff have confirmed.

A plant's status only changes after a staff member records a documented assessment. If you think a record is wrong, report it from the plant's card, or ask for a correction.
