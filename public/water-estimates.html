Area water estimates

Estimated TDS and pH of groundwater, by area

These figures describe untreated groundwater (tube-well / bore water) in each area. They are screening estimates, not certified readings, and they are not measurements of any filtration plant's output. Water in one street can differ a lot from the next.

Not a safety rating. TDS and pH alone do not show whether water is safe. Bacteria (E. coli/coliform), arsenic, fluoride, nitrate and heavy metals need a laboratory test. A high estimate here is a reason to use treated water and to test local sources, not proof that a specific supply is unsafe.

How to read these numbers

  • TDS below 500 mg/L: generally acceptable from a TDS point of view.
  • 500–1,000 mg/L: test full chemistry and microbiology.
  • Above 1,000 mg/L: high — avoid relying on untreated water for regular drinking.
  • Above 2,000 mg/L: very high / brackish — appropriate treatment is needed.
  • pH: 7 is neutral; drinking-water guidance commonly uses 6.5–8.5 as an acceptable operational range. pH does not determine whether water is safe.

Source and limits

Supplied by the site owner as “Estimated TDS and pH Values by Area — Faisalabad” (not a certified water-quality report), based on public Faisalabad groundwater studies and regional sources consulted on 26 September 2026. Published city/division evidence reports pH around 6.8–8.2 in one study, a mean of 8.55 in another dataset and 7.9–8.6 in division-level monitoring. No source measured every named locality; the per-area figures are reasoned estimates that must be confirmed by on-site testing.

For a specific plant, look for dated water-test results on its card. Where none are recorded, the card says “No verified test data available”.
