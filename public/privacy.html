Privacy

What we keep, and for how long

You can find plants without an account and without giving us anything. We only collect information when you choose to report a problem or rate a plant.

  • Reports & ratings

Searching for plants

The place you search for, or your device location if you choose "Use my location", is sent to our server only to find nearby plants. It is not stored and not written to our logs. Your browser asks your permission before sharing your location, and you can always search by name or pick a spot on the map instead.

The map itself is loaded from the map provider shown at the bottom of every page, which receives the usual technical information any website receives when it serves map images.

When you report a problem or rate a plant

  • What you tell us: the plant, the kind of problem, your description, when you saw it, and an optional star rating.
  • Your phone number, so staff can follow up and to limit abuse. It is stored encrypted, is visible only to authorised staff (every time it is viewed is logged), and is never shown publicly or to other users.
  • If you choose to share that you are near the plant, we keep only the distance between you and the plant — not your position.

Photos

Hidden information inside photos — including GPS location and camera details — is removed when you upload them. Photos are not public: staff review them, and a photo is shown on the site only if staff approve it for publication. Please avoid photographing people's faces.

How long we keep things

Not stored

Search locations and "Use my location" results.

2 years

Report text and photos are kept for 2 years, then redacted.

180 days

Phone numbers are erased 180 days after your last activity.

Ask for a correction or deletion

You can ask us to correct a plant record, appeal a decision about your report, or delete your phone number and the reports linked to it. Use your report reference and the last four digits of your phone number.
